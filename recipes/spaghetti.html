Spaghetti

Description

Spaghetti with meat sauce is a delicious and easy dinner for the whole family. It combines tender spaghetti noodles with a flavorful tomato sauce and ground beef.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 12 ounces spaghetti noodles
  • 1 jar (24 ounces) pasta sauce
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Grated Parmesan cheese (optional)

Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions, then drain.
  2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
  3. Add the chopped onion and cook for 3-4 minutes until softened.
  4. Add the garlic and cook for another 30 seconds.
  5. Add the ground beef and cook until browned and fully cooked, breaking it into smaller pieces as it cooks.
  6. Drain excess grease, then add the pasta sauce. Season with salt and pepper.
  7. Simmer the meat sauce for 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  8. Serve the sauce over the spaghetti and sprinkle with Parmesan cheese if desired.
